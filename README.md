# AI-Powered Interview Preparation System with Personalized AI Tutor
Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning)

Vasireddy Venkatadri Institute of Technology (VVIT)

Major Project | Batch ID: AIML-C-14

# Abstract
Interview preparation is often difficult for students and job seekers because they need to identify their skills, practice interview questions, understand their weaknesses, and find suitable learning resources.
This project proposes an AI-Powered Interview Preparation System with a Personalized AI Tutor. The system analyzes the candidate's resume and extracts important information such as skills, projects, experience, and role. Based on the extracted information, the system generates a personalized mock interview with questions related to the candidate's skills and background.
After the interview, the AI analyzes the candidate's responses to identify strengths and skills that need improvement. The AI Tutor then uses this information to generate a personalized roadmap that focuses on the skills the candidate needs to improve and recommends suitable learning resources and websites.
The system uses Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models, Natural Language Processing, Resume Parsing, Speech-to-Text, and Text-to-Speech technologies. The backend is developed using Python and FastAPI, with SQLite for database management and PyMuPDF and spaCy for resume processing.

# Detailed Plan of Action
The project workflow is divided into five phases.

Phase 1: Resume Analysis
Upload and process the candidate's resume.
Extract candidate details, skills, projects, experience, and role.

Phase 2: Mock Interview
Generate interview questions based on the candidate's resume and skills.
Conduct a personalized mock interview and collect the candidate's responses.

Phase 3: Performance Analysis
Analyze the candidate's interview responses.
Identify strengths and skills that need improvement.

Phase 4: Personalized AI Tutor
Analyze the identified skill gaps.
Provide topic-wise guidance and generate a personalized learning roadmap.

Phase 5: Learning Resources
Identify the skills that need improvement.
Recommend suitable learning websites and resources for developing those skills.
